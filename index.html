The game

Side-view action, nonstop combat

Gun Frenzy puts every fighter on the same battle plane, seen from the side, in 3D arenas with a perspective camera. Reading the fight is as simple as a 2D classic — left, right, up — but the action has the depth and weight of a modern shooter.

Inspired by Soldat and Worms Rumble, the focus is the fast duel: reach the ammo first, gain height, read your opponent and land the shot mid-air.

Mechanics

Everything that happens in the arena

Double jump & backflip

The second jump can become a backflip: jump against your aim and spin backwards to gain distance.

Wall jump

Touching a wall turns it into a springboard. The sideways impulse takes priority and changes the vertical fight.

360° aim

The character follows the cursor: torso and head track the aim while the legs keep running.

Crouch & slide

Crouch for cover or slide keeping your momentum — and leave the slide in a jump that preserves speed.

Health & ammo

Floating health and ammo pickups respawn at fixed points. Controlling them decides matches.

Bots with personality

Defensive, balanced or aggressive: each bot has its own distance, aim and reaction time — and fights other bots too.

Scoreboard with flags

Kills and deaths for everyone on screen, with each fighter's name and flag. Bots keep their identity for the whole match.

Ragdoll

When someone falls, they really fall: the body inherits the last velocity and collapses with physics.
